## What makes a website weird

A website is often considered weird if it goes against the usual conventions. Here are some things you can expect from a weird website:

- **Unique content** – usually includes strange animations, unusual topics, or quirky games.
- **Unconventional web design** – breaks away from standard layouts and aesthetics, such as eccentric color schemes, odd navigation, or abstract graphics.
- **Unusual purpose or function** – has a vague purpose or lacks practical use.
- **Quirky interactive elements** – encourage unusual user interactions, such as dragging elements around aimlessly or scrolling through an endless page.
- **Humor** – has humorous, ironic, or absurd elements meant to entertain or confuse visitors.
