***Jackie Chan Stuntmaster*** (titled ***Jackie Chan's Stuntmaster*** in [PAL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PAL "PAL") 's in-game menu) is a 2000 [beat 'em up](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beat_%27em_up "Beat 'em up") [platform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platformer "Platformer") [video game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game "Video game") developed by [Radical Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radical_Entertainment "Radical Entertainment") for the [PlayStation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_\(console\) "PlayStation (console)").[^6] [Midway Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midway_Games "Midway Games") released it in North America while [Sony Computer Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sony_Computer_Entertainment "Sony Computer Entertainment") released it in Europe.

It stars martial artist [Jackie Chan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jackie_Chan "Jackie Chan"), who fights his way past numerous villains in different locations across New York City.

## Plot

Jackie's grandfather (Fredrick) has been kidnapped and Jackie needs to get him back. This means fighting his way through New York City to get to him. Jackie also faces a number of different challenges in which the player must use a combination of moves to succeed. The game boasts 15 fully [3D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3D_computer_graphics "3D computer graphics") levels with different environmental obstacles to cross.

## Development

The game was showcased at [E3 1999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E3_1999 "E3 1999").[^7] While making the game, Radical Entertainment consulted Jackie at every point so as to give the game the feeling of a Jackie Chan film. This included using Jackie for [motion capture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_capture "Motion capture"), so that the character in the game was performing the same moves as the man himself. He also provided his voice for the game. Also, when the player completes the game with all of the golden dragons collected, a video is shown with Jackie talking about the game as well as [behind-the-scenes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Making-of "Making-of") footage during the motion capture.

## Reception

The game received "average" reviews according to the [review aggregation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator "Review aggregator") website [GameRankings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GameRankings "GameRankings").[^8] Jeff Lundrigan of *[NextGen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Next_Generation_\(magazine\) "Next Generation (magazine)")* criticized the game as "one of the least inventive beat-'em-ups in years" and concluded his early review in four words: "Jackie Chan deserves better." [^17] *[GameSpot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GameSpot "GameSpot")* gave it an average review two-and-a-half months before its release date.[^6] *[GamePro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GamePro "GamePro")* called it "a worthy workout that just lacks a 'wow' factor. It's a nice accompaniment to your Jackie Chan movie rental weekend. But if you're really into throwing fists and busting kicks, then Chan is your man." [^21] [^2]
