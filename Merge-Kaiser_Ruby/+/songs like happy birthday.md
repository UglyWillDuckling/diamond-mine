Depending on whether you are looking for a direct replacement to sing during a cake ceremony or a party anthem to set the mood, there are several types of songs like "Happy Birthday."

### **Direct Alternatives to Sing**
If you want a song to replace the traditional "Happy Birthday to You" during the celebration:
- **"Happy Birthday" by Stevie Wonder**: Often cited as the best funky alternative that can replace the traditional tune 【1】.
- **"For He's a Jolly Good Fellow"**: A classic, though some consider it similarly repetitive to the standard birthday song.
- **Cultural Variations**: Depending on the region, you might use **"Las Mañanitas"** (Mexico) or **"Joyeux Anniversaire"** (France).

### **Upbeat Party Anthems**
These songs are ideal for a birthday playlist to get people dancing and celebrating:
- **"In Da Club" by 50 Cent**: A modern classic known for the line "Go shorty, it's your birthday" 【1】.
- **"Birthday" by The Beatles**: A high-energy rock track perfect for celebrations.
- **"It's My Party" by Leslie Gore**: A timeless track about the emotions of a birthday party.
- **"Birthday" by Anne-Marie**: A more recent pop song focused on the feeling of growing up 【3】.

### **Emotional or Thematic Songs**
If you are looking for songs that reflect on aging or relationships on a birthday:
- **"Never Grow Up" by Taylor Swift**: A singer-songwriter's take on the process of aging 【2】.
- **"Happy, Happy Birthday Baby" by The Tune Weavers**: A melancholic R&B track about a breakup, sung from the perspective of someone writing to an ex on their birthday 【2】.
- **"Happy Birthday" by Sufjan Stevens**: Described as a "deep cut" that may lead to questioning mortality 【1】.

Here are simple, well-known songs that share the same qualities as "Happy Birthday" — short phrases, repetition, a narrow vocal range, and easy-to-remember melodies.

### What Makes These Songs Similar to "Happy Birthday"
According to music education sources, melodies like "Happy Birthday" are memorable because they use **repetition**, have **short and clear phrases**, and span a **narrow range** (about one octave), making them easy to sing and remember [^3]. The melody is short, repetitive, and sits comfortably under the fingers or voice [^7]. Traditional American children's songs are noted for combining "simplicity with catchy melodies, making them easy for young children to learn and remember" [^4].

### Nursery Rhymes & Children's Songs
These are the closest match in simplicity and singability:

| Song | Origin / Notes |
|---|---|
| **Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star** | Based on a French melody ("Ah vous dirai-je, Maman"); shares the same simple, repeating structure [^5] |
| **London Bridge Is Falling Down** | 17th-century nursery rhyme with a short, repeating melody [^6] |
| **Mary Had a Little Lamb** | One of the most common beginner songs; very narrow range |
| **Humpty Dumpty** | Short traditional nursery rhyme [^8] |
| **Row, Row, Row Your Boat** | A round with just a few notes |
| **Ring Around the Rosie** | Very short, repetitive melody |

### Traditional Folk & Sing-Along Songs
These have slightly more lyrics but share the simple, singable quality:

| Song | Origin / Notes |
|---|---|
| **For He's a Jolly Good Fellow** | A traditional song commonly sung at celebrations, very similar in function to "Happy Birthday" [^1] |
| **Auld Lang Syne** | A well-known traditional song sung at gatherings [^1] |
| **You Are My Sunshine** | Simple, catchy melody widely loved [^9] |
| **This Land Is Your Land** | Woody Guthrie folk classic with a straightforward melody [^2] |
| **When the Saints Go Marching In** | Traditional gospel/folk tune, easy to sing along [^2] |
| **Shenandoah** | American folk song with a gentle, simple melody [^2] |
| **The Parting Glass** | A well-known traditional pub song [^1] |

### The Key Shared Traits
All of these songs share the characteristics that make "Happy Birthday" so universally singable:
- **Short melodic range** — typically within one octave
- **Repetition** — phrases repeat so the melody becomes familiar quickly
- **Clear, short phrases** — easy to breathe through and remember
- **No complex intervals** — stepwise motion that anyone can match

If you want the absolute closest match to "Happy Birthday" in terms of function (a song sung by a group at a gathering), **"For He's a Jolly Good Fellow"** and **"Auld Lang Syne"** are the most directly comparable [^1].
