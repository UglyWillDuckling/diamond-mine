This is 100% re-creation of the original theatrical trailer, which hasn't been seen on DVD/Blu-ray/4K UHD Blu-ray. You could only view on VHS and LaserDisc.  
  
For this restoration I've used a film scan from an original 1994 35mm release print of the movie. This is the most faithful representation of the trailer and you can also see how the movie actually looked in it's theatrical run.
