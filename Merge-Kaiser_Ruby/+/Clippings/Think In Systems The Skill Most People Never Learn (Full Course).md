WHAT THIS COURSE COVERS  
  
Part 1 introduces the core diagnostic: how to tell whether a problem is structural or an execution failure, and why the difference matters before you do anything else.  
  
Part 2 teaches you to draw any system on a page. You will learn to identify variables, trace the links between them, and recognise the two loop types that drive almost every repeating pattern.  
  
Part 3 covers stocks and flows — the actual plumbing of any system. Once you can name what is draining and find the valves that control it, the right intervention becomes obvious.  
  
Part 4 explains delays and oscillations. Most bad decisions come from reading the wrong signal at the wrong time. These tools show you why, and how to stop.  
  
Part 5 maps the four classic problem shapes: the patterns that trap people who are trying hard and still losing ground.  
  
Part 6 gives you a sorting tool for problem type before you choose a method. The right technique applied to the wrong category of problem reliably fails.  
  
Part 7 introduces leverage. Five rungs, ranked by impact. Most people operate on the bottom two without knowing it.  
  
The capstone walks a four-question protocol on one real problem of yours from start to finish.
