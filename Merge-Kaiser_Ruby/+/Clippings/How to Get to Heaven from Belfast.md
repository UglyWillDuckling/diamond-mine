***How to Get to Heaven from Belfast*** is an Irish [comedy thriller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comedy_thriller "Comedy thriller") series for [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix") created by [Lisa McGee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_McGee "Lisa McGee") and produced by [Hat Trick Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hat_Trick_Productions "Hat Trick Productions"). The series premiered on 12 February 2026.[^1]

When three close friends from [Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belfast "Belfast") receive the news that the estranged fourth member of their teenage friend group has unexpectedly died, they travel to [County Donegal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Donegal "County Donegal") in the north-west of [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland "Ireland") to pay their respects. Strange events at the wake and a long-kept secret threatening to come out propel the trio into investigating their friend's mysterious death, but the amateur sleuths quickly find themselves in over their heads.[^2] [^3]

The eight-part series was created by [Lisa McGee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisa_McGee "Lisa McGee").[^4] In August 2023 the series was announced to be executive produced by Caroline Leddy, Liz Lewin, and [Jimmy Mulville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jimmy_Mulville "Jimmy Mulville") for [Hat Trick Productions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hat_Trick_Productions "Hat Trick Productions") and [Channel 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channel_4 "Channel 4").[^5] In March 2024, the series moved to streaming service [Netflix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netflix "Netflix"). [Michael Lennox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Lennox "Michael Lennox") is the director.[^6]

On 1 July 2024, [Roisin Gallagher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roisin_Gallagher "Roisin Gallagher"), [Sinéad Keenan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sin%C3%A9ad_Keenan "Sinéad Keenan") and [Caoilfhionn Dunne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caoilfhionn_Dunne "Caoilfhionn Dunne") were cast in the lead roles.[^7] Younger versions of the main characters were cast with [Emma Canning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emma_Canning "Emma Canning"), Emily Flain, Chara Aitken and Maria Laird fulfilling the roles.[^8] The cast also includes [Jeanne Ní Áinle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeanne_N%C3%AD_%C3%81inle "Jeanne Ní Áinle") as well as [Emmett J. Scanlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmett_J._Scanlan "Emmett J. Scanlan"), [Tom Basden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Basden "Tom Basden"), [Ardal O'Hanlon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ardal_O%27Hanlon "Ardal O'Hanlon"), [Michelle Fairley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Fairley "Michelle Fairley"), [Saoirse-Monica Jackson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saoirse-Monica_Jackson "Saoirse-Monica Jackson") and [Josh Finan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josh_Finan "Josh Finan").[^9] [^10]

Filming began in [Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belfast "Belfast") in July 2024.[^11] [^12] [^13] Other filming locations include the Harbourview Hotel (formerly Londonderry Arms) in [Carnlough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnlough "Carnlough"), ([County Antrim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Antrim "County Antrim")), ([County Louth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Louth "County Louth")), [Saint John's Point (County Down)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_John%27s_Point,_County_Down "Saint John's Point, County Down"), Mamore Gap and [Inishowen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inishowen "Inishowen"), [County Donegal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Donegal "County Donegal"), [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin "Dublin"), and [Malta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malta "Malta").[^14] [^15]

Hat Trick Productions also produced Lisa McGee's previous series *[Derry Girls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derry_Girls "Derry Girls")*. Actors Sinéad Keenan, Emmett J. Scanlan, Maria Laird, Peter Campion, Ardal O'Hanlon, Bronagh Gallagher and Saoirse-Monica Jackson worked on both series, as did several other cast and crew members.[^16] The *Derry Girls* mural in Derry appears in a scene in episode 8.[^17]

The series was released on Netflix on 12 February 2026.[^1] The age rating for the series is " [15](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Board_of_Film_Classification#Current_certificates "British Board of Film Classification") " in the UK [^18] and " [TV-MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TV_Parental_Guidelines#TV-MA "TV Parental Guidelines") " in the US.[^19]

The [review aggregator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Review_aggregator "Review aggregator") website [Rotten Tomatoes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotten_Tomatoes "Rotten Tomatoes") reported an 94% approval rating based on 32 critic reviews. The website's critics consensus reads, "Stretching its tone in different directions without ever snapping, *How to Get to Heaven from Belfast* is another triumphantly funny and proudly Irish series from creator Lisa McGee." [^20] [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic"), which uses a weighted average, gave a score of 79 out of 100 based on 12 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.[^21]

*How to Get to Heaven from Belfast* was praised by critics.[^3] [^22] [^23]
