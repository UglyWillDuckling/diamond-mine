I made this video for my Calculus 1 class during Summer 2020, but I'm also making it public for those who have wondered why the derivative of sine is positive and why the derivative of cosine is negative, and whether there isn't a more unified way to look at their derivatives.  
  
This proof is much more satisfying to me than a squeeze theorem plus trig identity proof, and I hope you like it! I always lean towards geometric proofs when possible.
