Naru, a skilled warrior of the Comanche Nation, fights to protect her tribe against one of the first highly-evolved Predators to land on Earth.

### personal

Phenomenal movie. Spectacular in almost every way.

From the cinematography and direction to the photography and performance. This movie nails it all.

A truly fresh take on the predator, the movie is set in an unexpected locale and uses this wonderfully to draw in the viewer and immerse her into a setting at once beatiful and terrifying.

The film is full of life and wonder. Georgus panoramic shots and vistas that go on for miles leave you breathless, enchanted by nature, its size and its beauty inviting you in its embrace.

You are left in a serene state only to be woken up by the action that is both quick and bloody, and precise and methodical. The scenes sometimes juggle between multiple things happening at the same time, still, even then it is difficult to find one thing that is lazy or misplaced.

(Expressions like methodical and attention to detail come to mind here.)

(Every minute of the film instills a sense of wonder and amazement as it transports the viewer to a different time and place. An unfamiliar place, and at the same time comfortable and inviting.)

(The score is also phantastic.)
