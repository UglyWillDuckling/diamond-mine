***My Neighbor Totoro*** ([Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language): となりのトトロ, [Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepburn_romanization): *Tonari no Totoro*) is a 1988 [Japanese animated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anime) [fantasy film](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fantasy_film) written and directed by [Hayao Miyazaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayao_Miyazaki) and animated by [Studio Ghibli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studio_Ghibli) for [Tokuma Shoten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokuma_Shoten). It stars the voices of [Noriko Hidaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noriko_Hidaka), [Chika Sakamoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chika_Sakamoto) and [Hitoshi Takagi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hitoshi_Takagi), and focuses on two young sisters and their interactions with friendly wood spirits in [postwar rural Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postwar_Japan).

In 1989, [Streamline Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Streamline_Pictures) produced an English-language [dub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubbing_(filmmaking)) for exclusive use on transpacific flights of [Japan Airlines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Airlines). [Troma Films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Troma_Entertainment), under their 50th St. Films banner, distributed the dub of the film. This dub was released in 1993 to United States theaters, and the following year on [VHS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VHS) and [LaserDisc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaserDisc) in the US by [Fox Video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_Video) in 1994, and on [DVD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DVD) in 2002. The rights to this dub expired in 2004, so [Walt Disney Home Entertainment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walt_Disney_Studios_Home_Entertainment) re-released the film on March 7, 2006 with a new dub cast.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Neighbor_Totoro#cite_note-:0-1) This DVD release is the first version of the film in the US to include both Japanese and English language tracks.

The film explores themes such as [animism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animism), [Shinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinto) symbology, [environmentalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmentalism) and the joys of rural living. *My Neighbor Totoro* received worldwide critical acclaim, and has grossed over $41 million worldwide at the box office as of September 2019; the film also grossed significantly more from [home video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Home_video) sales and [merchandise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merchandising).

*My Neighbor Totoro* received numerous awards, including the [Animage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animage) Anime Grand Prix prize, the [Mainichi Film Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainichi_Film_Award), and [Kinema Junpo Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinema_Junpo_Awards) for Best Film in 1988. It also received the Special Award at the [Blue Ribbon Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Ribbon_Awards) in the same year. The film is considered as one of the top animation films, ranking 41st in [*Empire*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_(magazine)) magazine's "The 100 Best Films of World Cinema" in 2010 and the number-one animated film on the 2012 [*Sight & Sound*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sight_%26_Sound) critics' poll of [all-time greatest films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Sight_%26_Sound_Top_50_Greatest_Films_of_All_Time).[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Neighbor_Totoro#cite_note-sight-sound-2)[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Neighbor_Totoro#cite_note-:2-3) The film and its titular character have become cultural icons, and made multiple cameo appearances in other films. Totoro also serves as the mascot for Studio Ghibli and is recognized as one of the most popular characters in Japanese animation.
