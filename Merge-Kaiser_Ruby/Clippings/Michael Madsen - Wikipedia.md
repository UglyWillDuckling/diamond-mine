**Michael Søren Madsen** (September 25, 1957 – July 3, 2025) was an American actor. He was widely known for starring in [Quentin Tarantino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino "Quentin Tarantino") 's films such as *[Reservoir Dogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reservoir_Dogs "Reservoir Dogs")* (1992), *[Kill Bill: Volume 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kill_Bill:_Volume_2 "Kill Bill: Volume 2")* (2004), and *[The Hateful Eight](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hateful_Eight "The Hateful Eight")* (2015). His other film roles included *[WarGames](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WarGames_\(film\) "WarGames (film)")* (1983), *[The Natural](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Natural_\(film\) "The Natural (film)")* (1984), *[The Doors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Doors_\(film\) "The Doors (film)")* (1991), *[Thelma & Louise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thelma_%26_Louise "Thelma & Louise")* (1991), *[Free Willy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Willy "Free Willy")* (1993), *[Species](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Species_\(film\) "Species (film)")* (1995), *[Donnie Brasco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donnie_Brasco_\(film\) "Donnie Brasco (film)")* (1997), *[Die Another Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Die_Another_Day "Die Another Day")* (2002), *[Sin City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sin_City_\(film\) "Sin City (film)")* (2005), and *[Scary Movie 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scary_Movie_4 "Scary Movie 4")* (2006). Madsen also voiced characters in video games such as *[Grand Theft Auto III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_III "Grand Theft Auto III")* (2001), *[Narc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narc_\(video_game\) "Narc (video game)")* (2005), the [*Dishonored* series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dishonored_\(series\) "Dishonored (series)") (2012–2017), *[The Walking Dead: Season Two](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Walking_Dead:_Season_Two "The Walking Dead: Season Two")* (2014), and *[Crime Boss: Rockay City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crime_Boss:_Rockay_City "Crime Boss: Rockay City")* (2023). Madsen had six children, including actor [Christian Madsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Madsen "Christian Madsen").
