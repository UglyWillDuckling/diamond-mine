**Mikey Neumann** (born April 16, 1982) is an American [YouTuber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTuber "YouTuber") and video game writer. He is known for the channel *FilmJoy*, which hosts [film criticism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_criticism "Film criticism") series *Movies with Mikey*. The series celebrates the positive elements of movies, and was nominated for the Outstanding Informational Series or Special [Emmy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Award "Emmy Award") in 2017.

Before his work on YouTube, Neumann worked in the [video game industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_game_industry "Video game industry") as a creative director and on games including [*Brothers in Arms*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brothers_in_Arms_\(video_game_series\) "Brothers in Arms (video game series)"), *[Borderlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borderlands_\(video_game\) "Borderlands (video game)"),* and *[Borderlands 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borderlands_2 "Borderlands 2")*. He worked at [Gearbox Software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gearbox_Software "Gearbox Software") for 16 years, ultimately earning the title "chief creative champion" before departing the company because of health problems.

## Career

### Video games

Neumann began working in the video game industry at the age of 19. He left college after one year to do [quality assurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality_assurance "Quality assurance") on [Gearbox Software's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gearbox_Software "Gearbox Software") [PlayStation 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PlayStation_2 "PlayStation 2") port of [*Half-Life*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half-Life_\(video_game\) "Half-Life (video game)"), becoming a full-time texture artist in 2001, a year later.[^2] [^3] He went on to write, voice act, and serve as creative director or associate creative director for games including the [*Brothers in Arms*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brothers_in_Arms_\(video_game_series\) "Brothers in Arms (video game series)") series, *[Aliens: Colonial Marines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliens:_Colonial_Marines "Aliens: Colonial Marines"),* and [*Borderlands*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borderlands_\(video_game\) "Borderlands (video game)").[^4] [^5] For *Borderlands*, Neumann provided [motion capture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_capture "Motion capture") and voiced the character Scooter.[^6] [^7] He also wrote [comics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comics "Comics") for the series, including the prequel *Borderlands: Origins*.[^8] [^9] [^7]

When [pre-orders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pre-order "Pre-order") for *Borderlands* became available, Neumann tweeted "Twitter me a screen of you buying the game and I'll play BLs with you and give you loot." When the tweet was picked up by gaming news website [Kotaku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kotaku "Kotaku"),[^10] the popularity of the tweet exploded, with more than 200 players tweeting their game receipts. As a result, Neumann spent more than 3 days playing the game, grouping up with each player and giving them in-game weapons.[^11] In 2014, Neumann gave the [keynote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keynote "Keynote") talk at [PAX Prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PAX_Prime "PAX Prime").[^4] Alongside developers from other game studios, he created a card for [*Magic 2015*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic:_The_Gathering_%E2%80%93_Duels_of_the_Planeswalkers_2015 "Magic: The Gathering – Duels of the Planeswalkers 2015"), titled *Chasm Skulker*.[^12]

In 2016 Neumann, then in the position of 'chief creative champion', was announced as the lead writer on the upcoming *[Borderlands 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borderlands_3 "Borderlands 3")*.[^13] He left Gearbox Software in June 2017, before the game's release, as a result of ongoing health problems, saying that "Everything is 1,000,000% amicable with Gearbox. I physically just can't do the work anymore", and opened a [Patreon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patreon "Patreon") account to crowdfund his YouTube videos.[^14] Neumann later assisted with the *Borderlands 3* trailer.[^15]

### YouTube

In 2010, Neumann created a [web series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_series "Web series") alongside [Anthony Burch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Burch "Anthony Burch") called *Anthony Saves The World.*[^16] He started a [film criticism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Film_criticism "Film criticism") series titled *Movies with Mikey* in 2014 on the *Chainsawsuit Original* YouTube channel (now titled *FilmJoy*) in collaboration with [Kris Straub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kris_Straub "Kris Straub").[^17] The videos focus on the positive elements of movies, celebrating and highlighting aspects of those movies Neumann enjoyed. He described the impetus for the series as a desire for people "to feel good about the things they like, as opposed to feeling attacked all the time" and he aims to bring something new to viewers' opinion of the film, such as framing [*John Wick*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Wick_\(film\) "John Wick (film)") in a [mythological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mythological "Mythological") context.[^18] After leaving Gearbox Software in 2017, Neumann began funding the series via [Patreon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patreon "Patreon") as he worked to become a full-time [YouTuber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTuber "YouTuber").[^19] 5 days after his Patreon opened, more than 1,300 fans were pledging money each month to fund his videos.[^17]

Neumann envisioned *Movies with Mikey* as being focused on films that were reviewed negatively in the 1990s and 2000s, providing viewers with a deeper appreciation for them. When the first videos received few views, he refocused the series. His video on *[The World's End](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World%27s_End_\(film\) "The World's End (film)")* received considerably more attention, including being shared and commented on by the film's director and cast.[^3] *Movies with Mikey* has since covered movies and television shows, including *[10 Cloverfield Lane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10_Cloverfield_Lane "10 Cloverfield Lane")*,[^20] *[Princess Mononoke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Mononoke "Princess Mononoke")*,[^21] and *[Stranger Things](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranger_Things "Stranger Things")*.[^3] The series was nominated for an [Emmy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmy_Award "Emmy Award") in 2017, in the *Outstanding Informational Series or Special* category.[^22]

## Personal life

Neumann had a [stroke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stroke "Stroke") while playing games at the [Gearbox Software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gearbox_Software "Gearbox Software") office in October 2011, resulting in him being hospitalized for nine days. Doctors diagnosed Neumann with [multiple sclerosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_sclerosis "Multiple sclerosis").[^2] [^23] Following [auto-immune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autoimmunity "Autoimmunity") failure as a result of his condition, Neumann was hospitalized again in 2017.[^7] [^17]

Neumann has identified as [asexual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asexuality "Asexuality").[^24]
