**Harvey Keitel** ([/ k aɪ ˈ t ɛ l /](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English") [*ky- TEL*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key "Help:Pronunciation respelling key"); born May 13, 1939) is an American actor and film producer, known for his portrayal of morally ambiguous and "tough guy" characters.<sup><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup> <sup><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup> He rose to prominence during the [New Hollywood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Hollywood "New Hollywood") movement, and has held a long-running association with director [Martin Scorsese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Scorsese "Martin Scorsese"), starring in six of his films: *[Who's That Knocking at My Door](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Who%27s_That_Knocking_at_My_Door "Who's That Knocking at My Door")* (1967), *[Mean Streets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_Streets "Mean Streets")* (1973), *[Alice Doesn't Live Here Anymore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alice_Doesn%27t_Live_Here_Anymore "Alice Doesn't Live Here Anymore")* (1974), *[Taxi Driver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxi_Driver "Taxi Driver")* (1976), *[The Last Temptation of Christ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Last_Temptation_of_Christ_\(film\) "The Last Temptation of Christ (film)")* (1988), and *[The Irishman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Irishman "The Irishman")* (2019).<sup><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-BFI-3"><span>[</span>3<span>]</span></a></sup> He is also known for his work with director [Quentin Tarantino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quentin_Tarantino "Quentin Tarantino"), portraying Mr. White in *[Reservoir Dogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reservoir_Dogs "Reservoir Dogs")* (1992) and Winston Wolf in *[Pulp Fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulp_Fiction "Pulp Fiction")* (1994).

Keitel received a nomination for the [Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_Award_for_Best_Supporting_Actor "Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor") for his portrayal of [Mickey Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mickey_Cohen "Mickey Cohen") in *[Bugsy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bugsy "Bugsy")* (1991). He won the [AACTA Award for Best Actor in a Leading Role](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AACTA_Award_for_Best_Actor_in_a_Leading_Role "AACTA Award for Best Actor in a Leading Role") for his performance in *[The Piano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Piano "The Piano")* (1993).<sup><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-4"><span>[</span>4<span>]</span></a></sup> Keitel’s other notable films include *[Blue Collar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Collar_\(film\) "Blue Collar (film)")* (1978), *[Thelma & Louise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thelma_%26_Louise "Thelma & Louise")* (1991), *[Reservoir Dogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reservoir_Dogs "Reservoir Dogs")* (1992), *[Bad Lieutenant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Lieutenant "Bad Lieutenant")* (1992), *[Imaginary Crimes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imaginary_Crimes "Imaginary Crimes")* (1994), *[Pulp Fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pulp_Fiction "Pulp Fiction")* (1994), *[From Dusk till Dawn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/From_Dusk_till_Dawn "From Dusk till Dawn")* (1996), *[Cop Land](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cop_Land "Cop Land")* (1997), *[Holy Smoke!](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Smoke! "Holy Smoke!")* (1998), *[National Treasure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Treasure_\(film\) "National Treasure (film)")* (2004), *[National Treasure: Book of Secrets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Treasure:_Book_of_Secrets "National Treasure: Book of Secrets")* (2007) and *[Youth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Youth_\(2015_film\) "Youth (2015 film)")* (2015).

Additionally, Keitel has starred in three films directed by [Wes Anderson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wes_Anderson "Wes Anderson"): *[Moonrise Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moonrise_Kingdom "Moonrise Kingdom")* (2012), *[The Grand Budapest Hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Grand_Budapest_Hotel "The Grand Budapest Hotel")* (2014), and *[Isle of Dogs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isle_of_Dogs_\(film\) "Isle of Dogs (film)")* (2018). From 1995 to 2017, he was a co-president of the [Actors Studio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actors_Studio "Actors Studio"), alongside [Al Pacino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Pacino "Al Pacino") and [Ellen Burstyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellen_Burstyn "Ellen Burstyn").<sup><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-5"><span>[</span>5<span>]</span></a></sup>
