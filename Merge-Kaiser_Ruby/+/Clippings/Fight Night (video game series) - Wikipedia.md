***Fight Night*** is a series of [boxing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boxing "Boxing") [video games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_games "Video games") created by [EA Sports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EA_Sports "EA Sports"). It follows on from their previous series *[Knockout Kings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knockout_Kings "Knockout Kings")*, produced for various platforms yearly between 1998 and 2003. The series was well received critically, with the PS3 version of *Fight Night Round 4* achieving a [Metacritic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metacritic "Metacritic") score of 88/100,<sup><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-mcps3-1"><span>[</span>1<span>]</span></a></sup> and several of the games topping sales charts.<sup><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/#cite_note-2"><span>[</span>2<span>]</span></a></sup>
