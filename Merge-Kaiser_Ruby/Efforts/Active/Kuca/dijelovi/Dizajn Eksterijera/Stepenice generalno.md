Personally in terms of each stair not feeling like a gym workout, staying below 8 inches is ideal. If the place is going to be used by the elderly, 5.5 to 6 inches might be ideal. 7 is a happy medium though most commercial spaces use 6.7 inches or 17cm or whatever

Also the “going” or how much horizontal distance between stairs.
I've been in a house with 7 x 9 risers and despite having underhang, it felt like each step was truncated a bit much. The stairs at work feel better but would benefit from a bit more going.
**The stairs at a nursing home are perfect for me. I'm 5 foot 6 with 29 inch inside leg. Not sure if thigh and shin length.**

**I think the ideal going should be around 11 or 12 inches.** 

A stair machine at the gym is 8″ x 10″ although consider it is designed to give you a workout. Going too short puts more effort on your quads. Too long rarely happens due to space limitations. I did build some outdoor stairs that were about 9 x 15 to get up a bank and they felt quite a workout but the shin was vertical on the next step and brought my glute muscles into play to even out the effort. With 25% shorter rise and going they would have been ideal.
