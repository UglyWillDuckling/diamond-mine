**Colony Wars is a 1997 space combat** simulation video game developed and published by [[Psygnosis]] for the [[PlayStation]]. 

Players complete space combat missions using preselected starfighters equipped with various weapons. The game features multiple paths of missions and outcomes, depending on the player's performance. 

It was followed by [[Colony Wars 2 Vengance (1998)]] , and [[Colony Wars - Red Sun (2000)]].
