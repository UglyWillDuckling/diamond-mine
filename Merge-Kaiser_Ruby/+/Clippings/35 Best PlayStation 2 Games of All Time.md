A s we conclude our list of the best PS2 games of all time, let’s take a moment to discuss The Simpsons: Hit & Run, a game that continues to stand out as one of the most cherished and memorable titles on the PS2.

For those of us who grew up glued to The Simpsons TV series, this game is an absolute treat. It flawlessly channels the show’s humour, and the visuals, with their enduring cartoonish charm, still, shine brightly even after all these years.
